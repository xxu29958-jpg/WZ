Things to try

  • Move slowly. Keep going. Then stop.
  • Sneak up on Kon.
  • Write a wish on the plaque, then pause.
  • Be perfectly still. Kon will try to make you flinch.
  • Drag across the sand to rake it. Draw a circle for Kon to judge.
  • Move the cursor slowly along the sand, then freeze. Foxes hunt.
  • Do nothing for a while and watch where Kon goes.
  • If a sparrow drops by, don't scare it off. Kon wants to say hello.
  • Boop the nose. Pet the head. Circle Kon, quickly.
  • Light the lantern and watch the sun go down. (Put it out for morning.)
  • Shake the maple. Tap the bamboo. Wait for the wind.
  • At night, look up. Catch a falling star, then make a wish. Say hi to the moon.
  • Come back tomorrow. Kon notices.

Nothing you type is read or stored. Kon only sees that you are typing.
